Need to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Wildish located?

Wildish Restaurant is at Capitol Twin Peaks, 24 Staple Street, Colombo 02, Sri Lanka — in the heart of Colombo’s dining scene.

Do you offer a buffet?

Wildish is an à la carte restaurant — we do not offer a buffet. Every dish is freshly prepared and fire-cooked individually, so you can order exactly what you like from our menu.

How do I book a table?

Use the reservation form on our website to choose your date, lunch or dinner, and preferred time. We will contact you to confirm your booking. You can also call us directly on +94 71 333 3401 or message via VIP WhatsApp.

Do you serve lunch and dinner?

Yes. Select Lunch (11:00 AM – 3:30 PM) or Dinner (6:00 PM – 10:30 PM) when you book; opening hours are daily 11:00 AM – 10:30 PM.

What kind of food does Wildish serve?

Seasonal dishes built on fresh ingredients, artisan coffee, and vibrant flavours — Sri Lankan and international influences, crafted by Chef Charith N. Silva and the Wild Cookbook team.

Is Wildish related to Wild Cookbook?

Yes. Wildish is the official restaurant experience by Wild Cookbook — the same creative kitchen behind our videos and recipes on YouTube with over 10.8 Million subscribers.

Where can I follow Wildish online?

Find us on Instagram @wildish.colombo, Facebook, YouTube @wildcookbook, and TikTok for updates, events, and behind-the-scenes content.
